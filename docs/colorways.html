xTool Machine Colors

Every style in the xTool card's editor: one for each xTool model, and one for each color a model is sold in. The card draws a body (the frame or case) and a window (the plexi or lid, behind the title); models that look alike share them. A new card gets its laser model's style. The photo columns are a 7 by 7 pixel average on xTool's own product photo shown beside them; the card's colors are close to them, but tuned to read well on a dashboard.

Notes and sources

  • Variants with the same look. The D1 Pro 2.0 has the D1 Pro's frame. The F1 Ultra V2 is a Class 1 version of the F1 Ultra. The F2 Ultra (Single) has the F2 Ultra's enclosure. The F2 Ultra UV (Class 1) has the F2 Ultra UV's enclosure, so it uses the F2 Ultra UV style. The Apparel Printer looks the same as the Apparel Printer Plus.
  • D1 series. The D1 is grey only. The D1 Pro and D1 Pro 2.0 come in red or grey, the two colors of xTool's D1 and D1 Pro risers, which match the frame (xTool calls them Red and Grey on its UK store, Golden Red and Metal Grey on its German store). MatterHackers sells the red D1 Pro. The D1 series has no enclosure, so its title sits on the theme's card.
  • P2 and P2S. Both come in grey and white, as the variants of xTool's US refurbished P2/P2S listing show. xTool's EU and UK stores call the grey one black.
  • S1. Black with a green window, or white with an orange window (xTool's US refurbished S1 listing calls them Black and Pure White).
  • Laserbox. The Laserbox Pro is white with a smoked lid, and the Laserbox Basic is black.
  • Retail Marker. The in-store version of the F1 Ultra. Its enclosure color can be customized for a store (xTool support, article 2735); the card draws its standard look.
  • Model names are the ones the xTool integration gives each device in Home Assistant (for example, "xTool F2 Ultra UV").
